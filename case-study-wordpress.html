01 / CONTEXT

Maintaining useful, consistent course pages

Course websites need clear page structure, reliable content, and layouts that adapt to different screen sizes. My work has involved improving WordPress course pages and troubleshooting the way content and front-end components behave together.

The work covered several related areas: Divi-based layouts, reusable page sections, Advanced Custom Fields (ACF), course display ordering, responsive behavior, and browser-based debugging.

02 / WORK COVERED

What I worked on

01

Page layouts & reusable sections

Worked with course-page markup and styling to support consistent layouts and reusable front-end patterns within WordPress and Divi.

02

ACF-driven content & ordering

Investigated dynamic content behavior and a course-ordering issue, including the relationship between category-level selections and the order shown in course cards.

03

Responsive behavior & debugging

Reviewed mobile interactions and browser-console output to investigate front-end issues and check behavior across devices.

04

Image delivery & performance review

Worked on responsive WebP image variants and srcset delivery, and reviewed performance findings to identify areas for further investigation.

03 / APPROACH

Debug methodically, change carefully

My approach is to isolate the affected component, inspect the relevant HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and content configuration, then make a focused change and test the result. For responsive issues, I check behavior at different viewport sizes rather than relying on desktop appearance alone.

For dynamic WordPress content, I trace where the data is managed and how it is rendered, so the fix addresses the source of the behavior rather than only its visual symptoms.
